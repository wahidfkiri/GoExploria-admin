Common Questions

Find Answers To The Most Common Customer Queries

What's included in a full car service?
A complete service includes oil change, filter replacement, brake inspection, tire check, battery test, and engine diagnostics.
Do you offer pick-up and drop-off service?
Yes! We recommend a full service every 6 months or 5,000–10,000 km, depending on your driving habits. Pick-up and drop-off is available.
How long does a standard service take?
Most standard services are completed within 2–3 hours. Enjoy our comfortable waiting lounge with Wi-Fi and refreshments.
Is your service covered by warranty?
Yes, we offer up to a 3-month service warranty depending on the type of repair. We provide upfront estimates so you know exactly what to expect.
Do you provide emergency roadside assistance?
Yes, 24/7 emergency towing and roadside repair support is available. We provide upfront estimates so you know exactly what to expect.
